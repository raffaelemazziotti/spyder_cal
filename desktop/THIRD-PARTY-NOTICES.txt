Spyder Cal contains open source software, including Electron (MIT), Python
(PSF), PsychoPy (GPL v3), NumPy and SciPy (BSD), Matplotlib (Matplotlib license),
PyUSB (BSD), libusb (LGPL), pyglet (BSD), Pillow and FreeType.

Package license texts are included alongside the bundled runtime and Electron.

archive. Third party components retain their own licenses.
